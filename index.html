How it works

  1. Contact me on a platform above.
  2. Send your brief: references, character details, and whether the piece is for personal or commercial use. Tell me here if you don't want it posted publicly.
  3. I review the brief and send a quote. I can decline any brief before payment.
  4. Full payment confirms your spot in the queue. I start only after payment.
  5. I send a sketch. After you approve it, I finish the piece.
  6. I email you the final files.

Terms of service

By commissioning me, you agree to everything below. If you order through VGen or commission.gg, the platform's rules come first for payment, refunds, and disputes. These terms add to them for usage rights, revisions, and scope. If they conflict, the platform's rules win. Orders through DM or Ko-fi follow these terms in full.

Before you order and payment
  • I can decline any request before payment, for any reason.
  • I add you to the queue only after we agree on the brief and I receive full payment.
  • Full payment is required before I start any work.
  • Fees charged by the platform or payment processor are not refundable.
Turnaround
  • Estimated completion is 1 to 14 days after I receive payment, depending on complexity and my queue.
  • No rush orders. I work in queue order only.
  • If something delays your piece, I will tell you.
Revisions
  • You get 3 revision rounds during the sketch stage. Each round can include any number of changes.
  • I fix my own mistakes for free (wrong color, misread reference, and so on). They don't count toward your 3 rounds.
  • After you approve the sketch, major changes are no longer possible. Extra rounds are quoted separately.
  • Changing the brief after approval (new outfit, new composition, new character) counts as a new commission.
Refunds
  • Before I begin the sketch: full refund, minus platform or payment fees.
  • Sketch sent but not approved: 50% refund.
  • Sketch approved, or work has moved to line art or coloring: no refund.
  • If I cancel the commission: full refund.
Usage rights
  • Personal use: display, save, and share on social media with credit to me, or use as a profile picture or in non-income personal projects.
  • Commercial use means anything that earns income or promotes something that does: merchandise, book covers or illustrations, monetized channels or streams, ads, logos, and game assets. Discuss it with me before work starts. It is priced from ×2 the final price.
  • If you use the work commercially without telling me, you owe the commercial rate retroactively and I may refuse future commissions.
  • Do not put my work into any AI tool, generator, or training dataset.
  • Do not resell my work, edit it and claim it as your own, or remove my signature or watermark.
  • I keep the copyright to everything I draw.
Portfolio
  • I may post the finished piece, with a watermark, on my portfolio and social media.
  • If you don't want it shown, tell me in your brief, before work starts.
Delivery
  • I send files by email only, after the work is complete.
  • Format: PNG, 350 dpi, 2000 to 3000 px on the long side. Tell me in your brief if you need a different size.
  • The default background is white. Ask for a transparent background before work starts.
  • I don't include layered files (PSD) unless we agree on it.
